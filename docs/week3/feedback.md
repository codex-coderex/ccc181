# Week 3 feedback: self-review of personas and wireframes

## Join screen

**Works well:** The form simple and straightforward, with each field having its own row with labels, required fields being highlighted, and "Get ticket" is a full-page button on the phone screen. Joanna carries her bag in one hand, so it takes just a couple of seconds for her to know what to do. It satisfies her rule 4 (big buttons and text, light page without heavy images).

**Problem:** I found no problems that the persona may have. The form is relatively clear and simple to understand. 

**Change:** None. I am keeping both Join frames because they already follow rules 4 and 5 of the persona, and the form has one job.

## My place screen

**Works well:** The number of the ticket is the biggest element in both frames. Below it, there is the persona's chosen service, “There are (NUMBER) people ahead of you” satisfies rule 1, the counter, and “Last updated (auto-refreshes every 10 seconds)” satisfies rule 2. Rule 3 is satisfied through the full-width “Leave queue” button right below the card, which makes it easily noticeable. The status is written in words, not only styled, which fits her accessibility needs. 

**Problem:** Rule 2 says the ticket status and her counter must be in large text. In the frames both are bold but small next to the ticket number. They are readable up close, but Joanna checks her phone one-handed, often at arm's length while she is out and about, so a bit more size would give her margin when she is called (visibility of system status). This is a minor issue.

**Change:** I will make the status and the counter larger. I am keeping the rest of the frames, because the ticket number, the position sentence, the last-updated line and the Leave queue button already follow rules 1, 2 and 3.

## Staff dashboard (FR-04, FR-05, Michael)

**Works well:** Call Next comes first on the Currently Serving card and takes up the full width of the phone screen, because of that, the action that Michael does  most frequently is easily accessible, and it fits rule 1 (keeping his counter busy). The waiting list comes right after the card in order, and each row displays the service and the ticket number, so Michael knows who is next and what service they want before he calls, adhering to rules 1 and 2 (rule 2, preparing in advance). The actions Mark Served and No Show are located within the card of the currently serving ticket (rule 3), so the no-show customer can be dealt with in one place. The laptop navigation menu is fully displayed and the actions are clear buttons with appropriate labels, which means that no hidden menus exist (rule 4).

**Problem:** I found no problem. The three frames (nobody being served, calling the next ticket, mark served) show the whole flow, and the first row of the list is always visible under the Call Next button.

**Change:** None. I am keeping all three staff frames on both phone and desktop because they follow the persona's rules 1 to 4 as written.
